# PostCardly

Turn your photos into beautiful, shareable postcards.

PostCardly lets you pick or capture a photo, apply a filter, choose a font and
frame, add a short message, then share the finished postcard or save it to your
gallery.

## Download

- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/hk/app/postcardly-photo-postcards/id6802721589?l=en-GB)

## Features

- Pick or capture a photo
- 8 photo filters
- 11 message fonts
- 5 postcard frames
- Share to social media or save to your gallery
